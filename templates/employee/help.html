Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?
If you need to reset your password, please contact the IT department or use the password reset link on the login page.
How do I update my profile information?
Go to your Profile page from the navigation menu. You can update your personal information, contact details, and department information there.
I'm not receiving verification emails. What should I do?
First, check your spam/junk folder. If you still don't see the email, you can request a new verification email from the verification page, or contact IT support.
How do I view event details and statistics?
Navigate to the Events page from the main menu. Click on any event to view detailed information including ticket sales, revenue, and customer data.
Can I access this portal from my mobile device?
Yes! The employee portal is responsive and works well on mobile devices and tablets. Simply use your mobile browser to access the portal.
What should I do if I encounter an error?
If you encounter any errors or issues while using the portal, please take a screenshot and contact the IT support team with details about what you were trying to do when the error occurred.
